[style b=false i=false size=14px color=unset bgcolor=unset]"Three of Diamonds" is not just a map, but a subtle challenge to reconsider morality. Behind the façade of a private academy, you will meet charming students, each one a masterpiece of deceit and hypocrisy. Detective Elliot arrives to investigate a murder, but soon that ceases to be the story’s focus. The "Three of Diamonds" Academy does not tolerate observers - it turns everyone into a participant in its grand performance. And Elliot, as befits a hero of an ancient Greek tragedy, becomes one of the most beautiful masks at this masquerade. 

This map is not about investigation. It’s about incompetence, unhealthy liberalism, and the wounds that never had time to heal.[/style]
